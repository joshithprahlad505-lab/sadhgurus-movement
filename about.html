ABOUT SADHGURUS MOVEMENT

Our Mission

Practical Transformation

"Our mission is to make self-transformation practical, accessible, and meaningful for modern individuals. Through teachings, books, discussions, and spiritual exploration, we encourage people to become more conscious, responsible, and aware in everyday life."

Our Vision

A Conscious Society

"Our vision is a society where people live with greater awareness, compassion, inner balance, and responsibility. A society where transformation begins within and naturally influences families, communities, and future generations."

The Founder

Vamsi Kiran

Guide, Teacher, Author & Mentor

Vamsi Kiran is the founder and spiritual mentor of the SADHGURUS MOVEMENT. Combining ancient meditative insights with modern psychology, he guides seekers to explore the depths of their own consciousness.

As an author of influential spiritual books like Karma Chekaram, Arishedvargalu, and Swapna Rahasyalu, he addresses practical issues of life, helping seekers decipher subconscious symbols and conquer behavioral obstacles with absolute responsibility.

His discourses focus on simple, logical self-observation rather than blind dogmas, encouraging individuals of all paths to realize their highest human potential.
