The Purpose

of
Sadhgurus
Movement?

In a world filled with information, many people still struggle to understand themselves. Stress, confusion, fear, emotional conflict, and lack of direction continue to affect modern life despite material progress.


SADHGURUS MOVEMENT was created to help individuals understand the deeper dimensions of their own lives. Rather than offering beliefs to follow, it encourages self-observation, self-understanding, and conscious living.


The movement explores practical areas of inner growth including karma understanding, dream decoding, meditation, emotional clarity, self-transformation, and the study of the Arishedvargalu—the six inner obstacles that influence human behavior.


SADHGURUS MOVEMENT is a non-religious platform open to people of every background. Its purpose is not conversion, but transformation. Through books, teachings, discussions, and spiritual exploration, seekers are encouraged to discover greater awareness, responsibility, and inner balance in everyday life.

Core Explorations

The Five Pillars of Practice

Karma Understanding

Explore the physics of actions, thoughts, and intent. Learn how karma shapes reactions and how to act with absolute consciousness and responsibility.

Dream Decoding

Decipher Swapna Rahasyalu. Your dreams are the subconscious communicating patterns, fears, spiritual guides, and emotional clarity.

Meditation

Settle into silent self-observation. A non-sectarian approach to quietening mental chatter, resolving inner conflict, and finding space.

Arishedvargalu

Study the six inner obstacles (Lust, Anger, Greed, Attachment, Pride, Envy) that govern human behavior and cloud awareness.

Self Transformation

Move from compulsive reactions to conscious actions. Cultivate emotional clarity, mental resilience, and balanced, everyday wellbeing.

Swapna Rahasyalu

Understanding the Language of Dreams

"Dreams often reflect emotional patterns, psychological insights, subconscious processes, hidden fears, unresolved experiences, and deeper dimensions of human awareness. By understanding dreams, individuals can develop greater self-awareness and gain valuable insight into their inner world."

Symbolism

Common Dream Symbols

Decoding frequent dream visuals like falling, water, and flying. These archetypes reflect psychological states and foundational shifts happening inside your subconscious mind.

Patterns

Repeating Dreams

Recurring dreams indicate unresolved conflicts or themes demanding your attention. Learn how to address the underlying root causes to allow integration and release.

Awareness

Spiritual Dreams

Highly vivid, clear dreams offering deep peace or profound experiences. They are connections with deeper awareness and are often anchors of inner guidance.

Release

Fear-Based Dreams

Nightmares or anxiety-ridden scenarios are your subconscious attempting to process fears and past shocks. Unpacking these dreams releases their emotional hold over you.

Direction

Guidance Dreams

Dreams containing profound advice, clear paths, or warnings. They tap into your own inner intelligence, providing intuition for critical waking decisions.
